GAINZOPE / PRE-LAUNCH RULES

Spin & fair-play rules

Reward features should be easy to understand before participation. This page explains what GAINZOPE will publish for the spin experience.

Launch requirement: Before the spin feature is available, GAINZOPE will publish the selection method or applicable odds, participation limits, eligibility, any deadline and the reward-crediting process. Those final rules will control the live feature.

Participation

The app will show whether spin is available to an account and any participation conditions before the user starts. Do not assume a spin or reward is available unless it appears in the app.

Reward selection

Final live rules will explain how a reward is selected and how the result is confirmed. Users should review those rules before participating.

Fair use

GAINZOPE may review activity to protect users and the programme from duplicate accounts, automated activity, manipulation or other invalid participation under the final rules.

Getting help

For a question about reward rules, contact support.gainzope@gmail.com.
