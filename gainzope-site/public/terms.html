GAINZOPE / PRE-LAUNCH RULES

Reward terms & value rules

These are the current GAINZOPE reward rules. Complete in-app terms will be available before Android launch.

Current value rule: 100 points = 1 GAINZOPE token. 1 token = ₹1 of eligible value. The app will show the available reward, eligibility and conditions before redemption.

Ways to earn

Eligible opportunities include daily spin, CPX Research surveys, rewarded-ad opportunities, tasks, offers and referrals. Points are credited only after an activity meets its stated requirements and the result is confirmed.

Using tokens

GAINZOPE is currently for India. Users sign in with an Indian mobile number and can use eligible tokens for available Indian mobile recharge discounts and gift card rewards. Limits, status and current requirements are shown in the app at the time of use.

Referrals

When a referred user completes their first valid task, the referrer receives 250 points and the referred user receives 150 points. Milestones are 2,000 points at 15 successful referrals, 3,500 at 25 and 10,000 at 50. Up to 10 successful referrals can count in a day. Invalid, duplicate or abusive activity can be reviewed or reversed.

Changes and availability

Activities, gift cards, offers, redemption options and eligibility may change. The live information displayed with an activity or redemption controls the current experience.

Support

Questions about points, tasks, surveys, referrals and recharge status can be sent to support.gainzope@gmail.com.
