GAINZOPE / PRE-LAUNCH RULES

Spin & fair-play rules

The spin experience should be easy to understand before anyone participates.

Launch requirement: Before spin is live, GAINZOPE will publish the selection method or applicable odds, eligibility, participation limits, any deadline and the reward-crediting process. Those final rules control the live feature.

Participation

Every eligible account gets one free spin per day based on Indian Standard Time. Valid task completions and programme milestones may unlock bonus spins. The app shows current availability and participation conditions before a user starts.

Reward selection

The spin experience may include Mystery Box rewards. Final live rules will explain how each reward is selected and how the result is confirmed. Review those rules before participating.

Fair use

GAINZOPE may review activity to protect users and the programme from duplicate accounts, automated activity, manipulation or other invalid participation under the final rules.

Getting help

For spin or reward questions, contact hello@gainzope.in.
