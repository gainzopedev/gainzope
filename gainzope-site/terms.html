GAINZOPE / PRE-LAUNCH RULES

Reward terms & value rules

The following describes the current GAINZOPE reward model. Complete in-app terms will be published before the Android app launches.

Current value rule: 100 points = 1 GAINZOPE token. 1 token = ₹1 of eligible value. The final app will show the applicable option, eligibility and conditions before a user confirms a redemption.

Ways to earn

Eligible earning opportunities may include daily spin, surveys, tasks, offers and referrals. An activity earns value only when its stated requirements are completed and the app confirms the result.

Using tokens

Eligible tokens may be used for available mobile recharge discounts and gift card rewards. Catalogue availability, limits, redemption requirements and any processing status are shown in the app at the time of use.

Referrals

Referral rewards are subject to the current referral conditions shown in the app. GAINZOPE may limit, review or reverse rewards connected to invalid, duplicate or abusive activity under the final programme rules.

Changes and availability

Activities, gift cards, offers, redemption options and eligibility may change. The live app rules and information displayed with an activity or redemption control the current experience.

Support

Questions about the programme can be sent to hello@gainzope.in.
